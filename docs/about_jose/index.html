About Jose

Jose teaches English online and in Peru.

Born in Lima, Peru and raised in Cleveland, Ohio, Jose brings a unique bicultural perspective to his teaching. His journey has been shaped by both South American roots and years of living in the United States.

Jose taught at Esperanza in Cleveland, Ohio, where he supported students in developing their English skills and achieving academic success, including professional communication and English for the workplace. That experience deepened his commitment to education and inspired his current work teaching English online and in Peru, where he continues to help students build the English they need at work.

Today, Jose teaches English in Peru, in person and online. He works with adult learners one on one and in groups. As a native English speaker who also speaks Spanish, he understands the challenges Spanish speakers face and tailors each class to the student's goals, whether that is work, travel or everyday conversation.

Jose is TEFL certified (Teaching English as a Foreign Language) through Maximo Nivel, an intercultural institute with locations in Peru, Guatemala and Costa Rica. Its TEFL course is a 4-week, 150-hour program that includes practical teaching experience.
